ABOUT ME

A brief introduction.

I'm Jie (Xander) Wang. I studied Biochemistry and Molecular Biology at UC Davis, with a minor in Bioinformatics. My interests extend far beyond science — from music, psychology, and philosophy to hiking, travel, and understanding how the world works.

This website is a personal archive of what I learn, what I think about, and what I discover along the way.
