ABOUT ME

A brief introduction.

I'm Jie Wang, a UC Davis graduate with a background in Biochemistry and Molecular Biology and a minor in Bioinformatics. I'm curious about life and the world around me, from the smallest atoms and molecules to the vastness of the universe.

I often find myself asking how things work and why they are the way they are. I enjoy learning the fundamental principles behind what I encounter, including how people connect, interact, and shape the society we live in.
