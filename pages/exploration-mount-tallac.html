Mount Tallac was not just a hike. It became a marker for the point where I started taking exploration more seriously — not as tourism, but as a way to test myself and see the world from a different scale.

What I remember

The altitude, the silence, the changing terrain, and the moment the landscape opened below me. The mountain did not answer any big life question, but it made the questions feel smaller.

Why it matters
