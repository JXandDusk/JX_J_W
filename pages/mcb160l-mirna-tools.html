microRNA Screen · Reporter Tools

Arabidopsis reporter / tobacco validation

Organized learning notes based on my course notebook, with recorded observations distinguished from interpretation.

Purpose

Build and evaluate a luciferase reporter tool that converts miRNA-dependent regulation into a measurable signal before using it in a genetic screen.

Skills Gained · Workplace applications

Cloning design

Skill practiced: Distinguish primer annealing sequence from assembly extensions.

Workplace application: Plan construct assembly and diagnostic checks.

DNA calculations

Skill practiced: Convert concentration, mass, fragment length, and molar ratio.

Workplace application: Set up reproducible cloning mixtures.

Reporter quantification

Skill practiced: Compare matched regions and distinguish own from comparison-group observations.

Workplace application: Assess assay behavior and variability.

Core principle

A functional miRNA/target relationship can suppress luciferase output. Comparing an intact target-site reporter with a resistant/mutant reporter tests whether signal changes are compatible with regulation. Gibson assembly joins DNA fragments with homologous overlaps; selection and diagnostic digestion are separate verification steps.

Workflow · What each stage contributes

  1. Plan the reporter construct and primers with assembly overlaps.
  2. Amplify the insert and linearize the destination vector.
  3. Clean and quantify DNA; calculate an insert:vector molar ratio.
  4. Assemble and transform, with assembly-negative and transformation-positive controls.
  5. Obtain plasmids from selected colonies and check diagnostic digests.
  6. Evaluate transient reporter behavior in tobacco leaves and quantify matched image regions.

Recorded-count example · Transformation efficiency

The positive-control plate contained 145 colonies from 9/10 of a recovery containing 5 ng of plasmid:

Estimated colonies = 145/(0.9) ≈ 161
Efficiency ≈ 161/0.005 µg ≈ 3.22 × 10⁴ CFU/µg

The 1/10 plate had 15 colonies: 15/0.1/0.005 = 3.00 × 10⁴ CFU/µg. These estimates correct for plated fraction; they are not a claim of Gibson assembly efficiency.

Assembly planning

Insert mass = vector mass × (insert length/vector length) × desired molar ratio

A 3:1 molar ratio is not necessarily a 3:1 mass ratio.

Recorded observations & interpretation

The notebook reports 8 colonies on the Gibson 9/10 plate and none on the Gibson 1/10 or assembly-negative plates. It records using a partner’s colonies for downstream work. One diagnostic digest was interpreted as approximately 3-kb and 1-kb fragments; another appeared incompletely digested. Own-leaf reporter ratios were approximately 1.234 and 1.005; a comparison group had stronger contrasts, approximately 2.48 and 2.27.

What the evidence can and cannot establish

Few colonies do not alone establish failed assembly, and incomplete digestion complicates plasmid verification. Keep partner-derived material and comparison-group imaging explicit. The own-leaf signal differences are variable and do not support an unqualified statement of robust suppression. Equal-sized regions, labeling, background, exposure, and biological replicates matter. The comparison-group notebook contains a numeric transcription discrepancy, so rounded ratios are used here. Continue to screen analysis →

Review questions

  • Why include both an assembly-negative and transformation-positive control?
  • Why are molar and mass ratios different?
  • What additional experiment would establish consistent reporter behavior?
