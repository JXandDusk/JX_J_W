DNA Methylation & Reporter Activity

Arabidopsis thaliana

Organized learning notes based on my course notebook, with recorded observations distinguished from interpretation.

Purpose

Connect altered methylation maintenance with reporter behavior using two independent evidence types: restriction/PCR analysis and leaf luminescence.

Skills Gained · Workplace applications

Epigenetic assay interpretation

Skill practiced: Understand how methylation-dependent digestion changes amplifiable DNA.

Workplace application: Interpret indirect molecular assays.

Image quantification

Skill practiced: Normalize a target band to a control and document lane identity.

Workplace application: Analyze gel data with traceable measurements.

Evidence integration

Skill practiced: Keep DNA-template evidence separate from reporter expression/activity.

Workplace application: Compare molecular and functional readouts without conflating them.

Core principle

Compare LUC6 and LUC6 met1 plants. In the course assay, methylation inhibits HhaI digestion, while GlaI digestion requires suitable methylated recognition sites. A cut within the target interval reduces amplifiable intact template. A genomic control band supports a relative target/control comparison. Leaf luminescence is a separate functional readout.

Workflow · What each stage contributes

  1. Prepare leaf genomic DNA and assess its quantity/quality.
  2. Digest each genotype separately with the two enzymes.
  3. Amplify the LUC target interval and control region.
  4. Measure target and control bands using consistent image-analysis regions.
  5. Compare leaf luminescence after reporter-substrate treatment.
  6. Ask whether molecular and phenotype readouts support the same methylation model.

Recorded calculation · Target/control ratio

The notebook’s LUC6 met1 + GlaI entry lists target area 13,178.903 and control area 427.849:

Target/control = 13178.903/427.849 ≈ 30.8

This dimensionless ratio reflects the PCR-band readout for that lane. It is not a direct luciferase-expression concentration, methylation percentage, or fold change between genotypes.

Recorded observations & interpretation

Reported ratios by the condition labels used in the notebook’s conclusion: LUC6 + HhaI ≈ 4.0447; LUC6 + GlaI ≈ 0.055; LUC6 met1 + HhaI ≈ 0.0447; LUC6 met1 + GlaI ≈ 30.802. The notes describe greater luminescence from mutant leaves. Together these observations are compatible with reduced methylation and reduced reporter silencing.

What the evidence can and cannot establish

Some table-side lane labels contradict the condition labels and final written summary; the condition assignment should be checked against the original gel map before treating the numbers as definitive. Restriction/PCR evaluates amplifiable DNA after digestion, not direct gene expression. Endpoint PCR is only semiquantitative under controlled conditions; saturation, digestion completeness, weak controls, and image background can distort ratios. Enzyme methylation requirements are site-specific; do not generalize GlaI to every methylated sequence.

Review questions

  • Why do the two enzymes give opposite expected patterns?
  • Why is a control PCR product needed?
  • How does leaf luminescence differ from a LUC/control PCR ratio?
