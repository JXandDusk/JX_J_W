Element 3 Services

A project journal of my experience at Element 3 Services, organized by work period.

Key Skills & Takeaways

My experience at Element 3 Services helped me develop practical skills in quality inspection, field service, team leadership, and clear documentation across different sites and equipment.

01 / QUALITY CONTROL

Inspect carefully and document clearly

Inspecting 50 ocean-shipped enclosures at the CATL site in Long Beach and carrying out battery quality inspections at Gotion gave me experience observing equipment condition and recording inspection work.

02 / FIELD SERVICE

Build practical repair experience

Battery enclosure replacement, battery repair, liquid-cooling upgrades, and fuse and relay replacement on EV charging equipment broadened my experience with different service tasks.

03 / TEAM LEADERSHIP

Support people while leading the work

As a Sub-Leader supporting four employees and later a Leader of three other technicians, I gained experience guiding teammates and coordinating on-site work.

04 / COMMUNICATION

Communicate with the people on site

Working with site personnel and fellow technicians helped me practice exchanging information and keeping communication clear during field assignments.

05 / DOCUMENTATION

Turn daily work into a useful record

Photo documentation, evening inspection reports, and daily progress reporting developed my ability to summarize what was done and communicate work status.

06 / ADAPTABILITY

Adapt across projects and responsibilities

Assignments in Long Beach, Chicago, Seattle, and Arizona exposed me to different work environments and responsibilities, from inspection and repair to team leadership.

What I take forward

I learned to connect hands-on work with clear communication and documentation: inspect carefully, contribute to repair tasks, support the team, and report the work clearly.
