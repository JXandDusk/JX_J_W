The Odyssey Period

Philosophy · 2026

There was a time when I believed life followed a clear sequence: school, degree, career, stability. After graduation, the coastline disappeared.

The open ocean

Perhaps this is the Odyssey period — a ship leaving the shore and entering an ocean with no visible end. There are many directions, and the fear is choosing the wrong one.

But drifting is also a choice.

Direction comes later

Sometimes you do not know where east is while the sky is still dark. You keep moving, collecting experience, meeting people, testing routes, and learning what matters to you. Only when dawn arrives does the direction become visible.
